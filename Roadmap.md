# Roadmap

Forward-looking items that are scoped but not scheduled. Nothing here is
committed; each entry captures intent, rationale and open questions so a
decision can be made quickly later. Ideas live here, not in the code.

An entry graduates by becoming a requirement (`requirements/REQ-NNN-*.md`)
or is closed with a reason.
